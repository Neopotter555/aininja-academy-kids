SPECIAL SAFETY NOTE · CODEX

Ages 10–12 design. The teacher operates.

Younger learners can plan prompts, predict the result, test the game, and suggest fixes. An adult must sign in, type or run the Codex task, approve every action, and review the result before learners use it.

AGES 10–12 No personal Codex account and no direct operation. Use an adult-led demonstration.
AGES 13–16 Direct use only with parent or guardian permission and active teacher supervision.
EVERY LEARNER Never share account credentials or enter names, faces, voices, locations, secrets, or private schoolwork.

For Thailand workshops, collect written guardian permission and provide a clear privacy notice. Re-check the service’s current age rules and local requirements before every cohort.

WHAT YOU NEED

Teacher preparation checklist

  • Team devices

    One laptop or tablet for every pair or team of three.

  • Teacher station

    One laptop and projector for stories and adult-operated AI demonstrations.

  • Browser

    Current Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox in normal mode—not private or incognito mode.

  • Printouts

    GOAL prompt cards, SAFE cards, engineer logs, and peer-test sheets from the curriculum.

  • Internet

    Required to open the hosted app and run a teacher Codex demo. Paper and offline builds can continue without it.

  • Accounts

    No learner account is needed. Use one teacher-approved account only when demonstrating AI.

SHARED-DEVICE RULE

Assign the same device every week

Session completion and the Final Quest checklist are stored only in this browser on this device. They do not follow a learner to another laptop and will reset if browser storage is cleared.

TEAM + DEVICE + BROWSER Example: Team Orange → Laptop 07 → Chrome
  1. Label each device and record which team uses it.
  2. Return the team to the same device and browser each session.
  3. Avoid private or incognito mode and do not clear site data during the course.
  4. Keep the paper engineer log as the backup record of learning.

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Keep people in control

  • Use only teacher- or school-approved AI services and follow current age and consent rules.
  • For ages 10–12, the teacher signs in and operates Codex; learners plan, predict, test, and improve.
  • For ages 13–16, direct Codex use requires parent or guardian permission and teacher supervision.
  • Use fictional or teacher-provided information in every demonstration.
  • Never enter a child’s face, voice, precise location, health details, or private schoolwork.
  • Do not use AI as a therapist, doctor, authority, disciplinarian, or private confidant.
  • Review generated material before showing it widely or publishing it.
  • Keep a complete non-AI path for consent, access, connectivity, or comfort differences.
