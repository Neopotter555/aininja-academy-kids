Same rhythm, every session

    Teacher demo

    Build challenge

    Learners make something in pairs or teams.

  4. 13 min

  6. 04 min

NON-NEGOTIABLES

Keep people in control

  • Use only teacher- or school-approved AI services and follow current age and consent rules.
  • Keep accounts teacher-managed for younger learners.
  • Use fictional or teacher-provided information in every demonstration.
  • Never enter a child’s face, voice, precise location, health details, or private schoolwork.
  • Do not use AI as a therapist, doctor, authority, disciplinarian, or private confidant.
  • Review generated material before showing it widely or publishing it.
  • Keep a complete non-AI path for consent, access, connectivity, or comfort differences.
